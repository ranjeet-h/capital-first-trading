# Capital-First Trading

An offline study book for learning to trade the Indian market (NSE/BSE, Zerodha Kite) with one rule above all others: **protect the capital first**.

This is an education book. It does not recommend any stock, strategy or broker action, and it never promises profit. Most beginners lose money; the aim here is to lose little, learn fast and decide with evidence.

## Where to start

1. **Track 0: Start here** - what trading is and is not, the capital-first rules, how to use this book.
2. **Track 1: Market foundations** - shares, exchanges, settlement, order books, circuits, indices.
3. **Track 2: Money and math** - costs, taxes, risk per trade, position sizing, drawdowns, expectancy.

More tracks (chart reading, Kite demo, indicators, strategy, testing, psychology, graduation gates) are still being written.

## How to use this book

- Read in order. Each chapter is short. Do the "Try it" exercises and the quiz before you move on.
- Demos on the page are for practice. Change the numbers and see what happens.
- All money examples are made up and for learning only.
- A small yellow **check** tag means a fact (a charge, tax rate or rule) can change. Confirm it on the official source before you rely on it.
- This book is still a draft. Some tools, such as the trade journal, are still being built.
