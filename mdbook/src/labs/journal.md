# Trade journal

Write your plan before you buy. Record what you did. Look at the numbers later.

Your journal stays on this device. It does not need the internet and nothing is sent anywhere. Because the browser can clear its own data, make a backup now and then from the Backup tab.

## How to use it

1. **Plan.** Write the entry, the stop-loss and your reason before you buy.
2. **Record.** After you buy, tap *I bought*. When you sell, tap *I sold*. You can sell in parts.
3. **Review.** Say whether you followed your plan and how you felt. This is the part that teaches you most.
4. **Look.** The Dashboard shows your results. With fewer than 30 closed trades, treat them as a first look, not proof.

*R* means the loss you planned. A trade that makes twice the planned loss is +2 R.

This tool keeps records. It does not tell you whether to trade.
