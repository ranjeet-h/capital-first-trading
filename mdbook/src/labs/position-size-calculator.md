# Position size calculator

Turn "how much can I lose" into "how many shares", before you place the trade.

Enter your capital, the share of it you are willing to lose on this trade, your entry price and your stop-loss price. The tool shows how many shares fit your rules, and what limits the number.

## How to read it

- **Loss if the stop is hit** is the planned loss plus costs. It stays inside your risk budget.
- **What limits the size** tells you if the risk limit, the position cap or your cash decided the number.
- **Things to check** lists limits you set that the trade breaks. It never says a trade is good or bad.

Long trades in the cash segment only. No short selling and no borrowed money.
