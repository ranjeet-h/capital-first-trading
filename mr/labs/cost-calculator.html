Cost calculator

See what one trade really costs on Zerodha, and how far the price must move before you break even.

Type a buy price, a sell price and a quantity. The table shows every charge. The rates come from Zerodha’s own charges page, and the date is shown at the top of the tool.

How to read it

  • Break-even sell price is the lowest sell price where you make no loss after costs.
  • On small delivery trades, the fixed DP charge is a large share of the cost.
  • Intraday costs look small on one trade. They add up fast if you trade every day.

This tool shows costs only. It does not include tax on your profit, and it does not tell you whether to trade.
