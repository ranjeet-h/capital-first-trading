Capital-First Trading

Gate G3 Kite readiness

Hook

A plan looks right on screen. The order type is correct, but the product type is set to the wrong option. The error is small to see and large in effect. This gate checks that you know what each Kite feature, order type and product type does. It also checks the common execution mistakes. Passing it does not mean you should trade.

The idea

This gate has two parts. You must pass both.

  1. Demo checklist. Every item from the Kite chapters must be ticked. Each item has a short reflection box. You write what you did and what you noticed. An empty reflection fails that item.
  2. Execution quiz. You need at least 90% correct on the questions drawn for you.

This measures educational competence. It is not proof of profitability. Nothing here requires or recommends trading with real money.

Some points to keep in mind:

  • This checklist is self-attested. You tick it yourself. The book cannot check your Kite demo.
  • Zerodha does not offer demo accounts for paper trading. It does offer a demo version of Kite that uses dummy data, at kite-demo.zerodha.com/dashboard. Use it to practise the screens, not to test a strategy. (As of 11 Nov 2026, Zerodha support. Check Zerodha support for changes.)
  • Any Kite screen you see in this book is a recreated illustration. It is dated, and menus and names may change. The Kite screens in this book were last checked against Zerodha’s public pages on 11 Nov 2026.
  • The quiz is retakeable, like the other gates. You can re-tick the checklist at any time. Each change is logged with its date.
  • Nothing in this gate asks you to place an order. This book never places orders for you.

Your ticks, reflections, quiz results and dates stay on your device.

Worked example

Hypothetical. The execution quiz draws 10 questions. The pass mark is 90%.

90% of 10 is 9. So you need 9 correct answers.

Correct answersScoreResult
99 / 10 = 90%Passed the quiz part
88 / 10 = 80%Not passed

Now the checklist. Suppose you have ticked all 9 items you did in the demo. But one reflection box is empty. That item fails, so the checklist part is not complete. The quiz score does not make up for it.

Try it

Guided exercise

Task. Hypothetical. The execution quiz draws 20 questions. How many correct answers are needed to reach 90%?

Hints:

  1. Multiply 20 by 0.90.
  2. Check the result. Divide it by 20.
  3. Test one fewer correct answer. Does it still reach 90%?

Answer: 20 x 0.90 = 18. Check: 18 / 20 = 90%. One fewer, 17 / 20 = 85%, is not enough. So 18 correct answers are needed.

Independent exercise

Task. Hypothetical. The execution quiz draws 30 questions. How many correct answers are needed to reach 90%? Then say what happens if one checklist item has an empty reflection, even though its box is ticked.

Check your answer
  • 30 x 0.90 = 27. Check: 27 / 30 = 90%. One fewer, 26 / 30 = 86.7%, is not enough. So 27 correct answers are needed.
  • The item fails. An empty reflection fails the item, even when the tick is in place. The checklist is not complete until that reflection has words.

All figures are hypothetical.

Quiz

Paper task

Mode: paper for the reflections. Use kite_demo only to look at the screens. Write your reflections in your journal. If the journal is not ready, use paper.

  1. Open Zerodha’s demo version of Kite (kite-demo.zerodha.com/dashboard) to look at the screens.
  2. Work through the checklist items one at a time.
  3. For each item, write a short reflection. Say what you did and what you noticed.
  4. Tick an item only after you have done it.
  5. Do not place any real order. This chapter is for practice only.

Limits and common mistakes

  • The book cannot check your demo work. Your tick is your own word.
  • The demo uses dummy data. Its prices and fills do not match the real market.
  • Kite menus and names may change. Check what you see against the current screen.
  • A pass shows you can answer the questions. It does not show you can place a trade safely with real money.
  • The quiz is at 90%, so one wrong answer in ten is still allowed. Read the explanation for every miss.

Remember

  • Every checklist item needs a tick and a reflection with words in it.
  • The quiz needs at least 90% correct.
  • The checklist is self-attested. The book cannot check it.
  • Dummy data in a demo does not show real market prices.
  • Passing this gate does not mean you should trade.
